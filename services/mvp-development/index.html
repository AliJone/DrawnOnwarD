    How an MVP build runs

    1. 01Roadmap, free, within one business day: you describe the product; we return scope, timeline and a budget range you can take to your co-founder or investors.
    2. 02Scoping: user flows, data model, integrations and the cut line for version one. The fixed quote and the milestones are agreed here.
    3. 03Design: a clickable prototype of every screen in the MVP, approved by you before engineering starts.
    4. 04Build: milestone by milestone, with a live demo every week and QA from the first milestone, so course corrections happen while they are cheap.
    5. 05Launch: production deployment, monitoring, documentation and a handover session. The repository has been yours all along.
    6. 06After launch: a support arrangement if you want one, or a clean handover to your own team.

    You own the code, the repository and the IP from day one

    You have access to the repository from day one and own 100% of the code, designs and intellectual property from the first commit, not after the final invoice. If you prefer, the repository lives in your own GitHub organisation and our engineers work in it as collaborators, so removing our access takes one click and nothing breaks.

    Payment is by milestone, after you have seen that milestone working in a live demo. Milestones and dates are agreed with the fixed quote. NDA on request. We take no equity and ask for none.

    Already have a prototype?

    If you have a Lovable, Bolt or v0 prototype, or a half-built MVP from another team, the roadmap starts with an audit of the code and the accounts: repository, hosting, domains, secrets. We then quote finishing it or rebuilding it and tell you which is cheaper. Taking a prototype to production usually means authentication done properly, row-level security on the database, secrets out of the client, CI/CD, monitoring and backups, and it usually fits the Scoped build band.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does MVP development cost?
    At DrawnOnwarD, a B2B SaaS MVP costs $15,000 to $40,000. A scoped build of one core workflow costs $5,000 to $15,000, and a data or API platform $40,000 to $80,000. The number is fixed in a quote before any code is written.
    How long does it take to build an MVP?
    8 to 12 weeks for a B2B SaaS MVP, 4 to 6 weeks for a scoped build, and 12 to 16 weeks for a data or API platform. The scope agreed in the roadmap sets the timeline, and you see progress in a live demo every week.
    What is in the free roadmap?
    Scope (what version one does and does not do), a timeline, a budget range and the stack we recommend, within one business day of your message. It is yours to keep, and to take to other agencies if you want to compare.
    Who exactly works on my MVP?
    A senior tech lead, a full-stack engineer, a product designer and a QA engineer, with DevOps and a certified project manager shared across projects. The engineers on your first call are the engineers who build; there are no juniors and no hand-offs.
    Do I own the code?
    Yes. 100% of the code, designs and IP from the first commit, with repository access from day one. NDA on request, and no equity.
    What stack do you use?
    Next.js, Nuxt, React or Vue on Node.js, Go or .NET for web; React Native or Flutter for mobile. We pick the stack an in-house team can hire for and take over without a rewrite.
    Can you take over a prototype or a half-built MVP?
    Yes. We audit the code and the accounts (repository, hosting, domains, secrets) first, then quote either finishing it or rebuilding it, and we tell you which is cheaper.
    How do payments and changes work?
    You pay by milestone, after a live demo of the work in that milestone. Changes to the agreed scope are quoted before they are built, so the fixed price never grows silently; small adjustments inside the scope are part of the build.
