    Hiring a senior engineer in the US takes months and costs a median $135,980 a year in salary alone (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2025). Marketplaces are faster, but you vet, manage and replace the freelancer yourself. Tech Leads for Hire sits between the two: a vetted senior engineer or tech lead from our own team, working inside yours, with us accountable for the fit.

    What you get

    • Engineers from our own 10-person team, not a marketplace: 4 to 9 years of experience, 8 on average; tech leads with 7 years or more
    • A shortlist within 5 business days, interviews with you, and a start within two weeks of your decision
    • Part-time (20 hours a week) or full-time (40 hours a week), month to month; change hours or stop with two weeks' notice
    • Live with your team 7 pm to midnight Pakistan time (US Eastern mornings, European afternoons), asynchronous in your tools the rest of the day
    • Replacement guarantee: if the engineer is not the right fit in the first two weeks, we replace them at no charge
    • One monthly invoice per engineer, quoted per engagement. No subscription, no placement fee
    • Your repository, your tools, your process. NDA on request

    How a placement runs

    1. 01Tell us the role: stack, seniority, hours, and what the first 90 days must deliver.
    2. 02Shortlist within 5 business days: one to three engineers from our team, with relevant work you can see and a monthly rate.
    3. 03You interview and choose. The engineer starts within two weeks of your decision.
    4. 04Onboarding week: access, codebase walkthrough, first ticket shipped.
    5. 05Monthly check-in with you on scope and fit. Extend, change hours, or hand over with two weeks' notice.

    Should you just hire one senior engineer instead of an agency?

    Often, yes, and that is what this service is: one senior engineer embedded in your team, with no project manager in between and no agency overhead. If you already have a technical co-founder or a lead who can direct the work, a tech lead for hire is usually the cheaper and faster option, and the roadmap will say so.

    Choose an MVP build instead when nobody on your side can manage engineering day to day, when you need design, QA and DevOps as well as code, or when you want a fixed price for a fixed scope. One engineer cannot be a product designer, a QA engineer and a DevOps engineer at once; a four-person MVP team can.

    Choose a full-time hire when you have 12 months of runway for the salary, the product has paying customers, and you want someone whose equity is in your company. Teams often start with a tech lead for hire and recruit their own lead once the product has revenue; we hand over cleanly when that happens.

    What a tech lead for hire does in your team

    • Owns architecture and code review for their area, and ships code themselves: this is a hands-on role, not an advisory one
    • Turns founder priorities into tickets, estimates and a weekly plan
    • Mentors your junior and mid-level developers
    • Sets up or fixes CI/CD, environments, monitoring and release discipline
    • Reports to you or your CTO, in your tools

    Stacks we place engineers in

    Node.js, Next.js, Nuxt, React, Vue, Go, .NET, React Native, Flutter, cloud infrastructure and CI/CD. If your stack is not on this list, say so in the first message and we will tell you quickly whether we have the right person.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is staff augmentation?
    Adding engineers from an outside company to your own team, under your direction, for as long as you need them. You get the capacity without recruiting, and without handing the project to an agency.
    How senior are the engineers?
    4 to 9 years of professional experience, 8 on average; tech leads have 7 or more. They are members of our team with work you can see, not profiles from a marketplace.
    How fast can someone start?
    A shortlist within 5 business days of your brief, and a start within two weeks of your decision.
    What hours will they work?
    Part-time is 20 hours a week, full-time 40. Every engagement includes live overlap from 7 pm to midnight Pakistan time (US Eastern mornings, European afternoons); the rest is asynchronous in your tools.
    How much does it cost?
    A monthly rate per engineer, set by role and hours and quoted per engagement within one business day. There is no subscription and no placement fee.
    What if it is not working out?
    Tell us within the first two weeks and we replace the engineer at no charge. After that, change hours or end the engagement with two weeks' notice.
    Who owns the work?
    You do. The engineer works in your repository and your tools, and everything they produce is yours. NDA on request.
    Is this cheaper than an agency build?
    For ongoing work inside an existing team, yes: you pay for one engineer, not a project team. For a fixed-scope product with nobody to manage it, an MVP build at a fixed price is usually cheaper overall. The roadmap says which fits.
