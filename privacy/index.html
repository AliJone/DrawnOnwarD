    Privacy Policy

    Last updated 7 November 2026.

    1. Who we are

    DrawnOnwarD is a software agency based in Pakistan. Contact: info@drawnonward.org.

    2. What we collect

    When you use the contact form we receive the name, email address and message you type, so that we can reply. If you email us, we keep the correspondence. We do not ask for payment details on this site.

    3. How we use it

    To answer your enquiry, prepare your project roadmap and, if you become a client, to deliver the work. We do not sell personal data and we do not send marketing email without your consent.

    4. Who else sees it

    Form submissions are delivered to us through a form-handling provider and our email provider. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. If we add analytics, we will name the provider here.

    5. Cookies

    This site sets no cookies of its own. Third-party services named above may set their own; your browser settings let you block or delete them.

    6. How long we keep it

    Enquiries for as long as needed to respond and, for clients, for the duration of the engagement and the legal retention period after it.

    7. Your rights

    You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use, by emailing info@drawnonward.org.

    8. Changes

    We will post any change here with a new date.
