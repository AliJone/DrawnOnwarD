    What an MVP costs

    Three bands cover most of what founders bring us. Every project gets a fixed quote after a free roadmap and is paid by milestone.

    1. $5k–15k

      Scoped build

      One core workflow, a prototype that must go to production, or a focused web or mobile app.

      Scoping roadmap, design, build, QA and deployment. Typically 4 to 6 weeks.

    2. $15k–40k

      B2B SaaS MVP

      The first sellable version of a SaaS product.

      Authentication, roles, billing, multi-tenant data, one integration and an admin view. Typically 8 to 12 weeks.

    3. $40k–80k

      Data or API platform

      Products where the engine is the product.

      Public API with auth and rate limits, data pipelines, dashboards and the SaaS layer around them. Typically 12 to 16 weeks.

    Each MVP team pairs a senior tech lead with a full-stack engineer, a product designer and a QA engineer. DevOps and a certified project manager are shared across projects. Tech leads for hire are priced per engagement: ask us.
